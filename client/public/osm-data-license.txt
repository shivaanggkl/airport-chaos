PROVENANCE

Dallas-Fort Worth
- Geographic bounds: south 32.5506, west -97.064, north 33.0028, east -96.53.
- The checked-in dataset does not record its provider, source URL, download date, OSM snapshot date, extraction date, or generated date.
- Current generation tooling uses a Geofabrik Texas PBF, osmium, scripts/preprocess-osm.mjs, scripts/chunk-dallas-data.mjs, and scripts/generate-dallas-map.mjs. This describes the current reproducible pipeline, not unrecorded history for the checked-in dataset.

Milwaukee
- City origin: 43.0389, -87.9065. Configured game-world coverage is 12 km by 12 km.
- Configured generation extent: south 42.9591098788, west -87.9627911294, north 43.0676678668, east -87.8153034541. Historical source-extract bounds were not separately recorded.
- The checked-in dataset does not record its provider, source URL, download date, OSM snapshot date, extraction date, or generated date.
- Its compact data format is produced by scripts/preprocess-osm.mjs; the historical upstream provider and acquisition process were not recorded.
