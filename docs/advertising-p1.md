## Creative standard for later operator-approved artwork

- Compose separately for each existing placement aspect ratio. Keep logo/name key content at least **8% inside each edge** of the visible area; decorative borders may sit outside this margin. One logo or symbol, one company name (preferably at most 28 characters), and an optional tagline (preferably at most 42 characters). Reject a name that cannot remain legible instead of shrinking it indefinitely. No paragraph copy, phone number, QR code, fake game prompt, or clickable flight-world control.
- Use clear contrast and restrained brightness in Day, Dusk, and Evening. Near LOD may show logo, name, tagline; medium prioritizes logo/name; far prioritizes a recognizable mark or name and may omit the tagline. Judge legibility at realistic flight distances. Do not distort the source image to fit a different ratio or clip key content.
- Future operator ingestion contract: static PNG/JPEG/WebP, initially at most 1 MiB compressed; verify decoded dimensions, aspect ratio, file signature, and content hash/version; reject oversize/decode bombs. Host approved files on the game origin. No executable SVG/HTML/JavaScript, third-party pixels, public uploader, or advertiser-defined placement coordinates. Reuse the existing cached canvas textures/materials and 1024/512/256 maximum LOD widths; no per-frame texture creation.
- This phase uses the existing local Airport Chaos logo and house colors on reviewed signs. The existing portrait tower wrap now uses a compact stacked house composition in the same cached renderer, avoiding horizontally stretched text. No external brand or new billboard geometry was added.
